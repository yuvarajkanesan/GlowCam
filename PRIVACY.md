# GlowCam Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-03_

GlowCam is a camera and photo-editing app that works entirely on your phone.

## What the app does with your data

- **Camera and microphone** are used only to show the live preview and to record the photos and videos you take.
- **Face and person detection** (for beauty effects, makeup, filters and background replace) run on the device. No image, video or face data is uploaded, stored on a server, or shared with anyone.
- **Photos and videos** are saved to `Pictures/GlowCam` and `Movies/GlowCam` on your phone. You can delete them at any time from the in-app gallery or any gallery app.
- **Location** (optional): if you turn on "Location tag" in Settings, the phone's last known location is written into new photos' EXIF data. It is off by default. The location is never sent anywhere by GlowCam; it travels only inside a photo if you share that photo.
- **Saved looks and settings** are stored on the device in the app's private storage.

## What the app does not do

- No account, sign-in or registration.
- No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs that send data.
- No internet access is required; the app does not request the INTERNET permission.

## Crash reports

If the app crashes, a short report (error trace, device model, Android version) is saved on your phone only. It is never sent automatically. You can choose to share it from Settings > About > Share last crash report.

## Sharing

Sharing to WhatsApp, Instagram, Snapchat or any other app happens only when you tap a share button. Only the photo or video you selected is passed to that app, which then handles it under its own privacy policy.

## Permissions used

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Camera | Live preview, photos, video |
| Microphone | Sound in videos |
| Location (optional) | Photo location tag, only if you enable it |
| Storage (Android 9 and older only) | Saving photos to the gallery |

## Children

GlowCam does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

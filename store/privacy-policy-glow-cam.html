GlowCam Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

GlowCam ("the app", package com.glowcam) is a camera and photo-editing app that works entirely on your phone. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: GlowCam does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

Information the app does not collect

  • No account, sign-in or registration.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking, and no third-party SDKs that send data.
  • No internet access: the app does not request the INTERNET permission, so nothing can be uploaded.

How the app uses your phone's features

  • Camera and microphone are used only to show the live preview and to record the photos and videos you take.
  • Face and person detection (used for beauty effects, makeup, filters and background replace) runs on the device. No image, video or face data leaves your phone.
  • Photos and videos are saved to Pictures/GlowCam and Movies/GlowCam on your phone. You can delete them at any time from the in-app gallery or any gallery app.
  • Location (optional): if you turn on "Location tag" in Settings, the phone's last known location is written into new photos' EXIF data. It is off by default. GlowCam never sends the location anywhere; it travels only inside a photo if you share that photo.
  • Saved looks and settings are stored on the device in the app's private storage.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, a short report (error trace, device model, Android version) is saved on your phone only. It is never sent automatically. You can choose to share it yourself from Settings > About > Share last crash report.

Sharing

Sharing to WhatsApp, Instagram, Snapchat or any other app happens only when you tap a share button. Only the photo or video you selected is passed to that app, which then handles it under its own privacy policy.

Children

GlowCam does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Data deletion

Because no data is collected or kept by us, there is nothing for us to delete. Photos and videos you made stay on your phone until you delete them; uninstalling the app removes its settings and saved looks.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the date above will change.

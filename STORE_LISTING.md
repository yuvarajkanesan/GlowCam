## Full description (max 4000)

GlowCam is a beauty camera that works live: see smooth skin, brighter looks, slimmer face, bigger eyes, makeup and filters in the viewfinder before you take the shot. Everything runs on your phone. No account, no ads, no uploads.

LIVE BEAUTY
- Smooth skin, brighten, slim face, big eyes and jaw shape, each with its own slider
- Makeup: lipstick, blush, eyebrows and eye shadow in many colours
- Hold the Compare button to see before and after

26+ FILTERS AND LOOKS
- Warm, cool, film, vintage, K-beauty soft glow and black and white
- Premium subject filters: Color Splash, Portrait Blur, Spotlight, Reverse Splash, Neon Duotone and more, which keep you sharp while the background changes
- One-tap Looks such as Natural Glow, Korean Soft and Glam Night, plus your own saved looks
- Replace the background with a colour, a gradient or a soft blur

PRO CAMERA CONTROLS
- Pinch to zoom with 0.6x, 1x, 2x, 3x, 5x and 10x lens buttons
- Tap to focus and drag to set brightness; long-press to lock focus and exposure
- HDR and Night modes
- Pro mode: manual ISO, shutter speed and white balance
- Sharp mode: image stabilisation, better noise and edge quality, and a hold-steady warning
- Press and hold the shutter to keep shooting
- Original quality mode saves the camera's untouched JPEG
- Choose your photo size in megapixels from the top bar
- Grid, level and histogram guides
- Photo, video up to 4K, timer, touch-to-shoot, screen-glow flash for selfies

FULL PHOTO EDITOR
- Crop, rotate, straighten, brightness, contrast, saturation, blur and vignette
- Retouch: remove blemishes, whiten teeth, fix dark circles and red-eye
- Add text and stickers
- Make collages from 2 to 6 photos
- Undo and redo, and save in high resolution

SHARE IN ONE TAP
- Share to WhatsApp, Instagram, Snapchat and any other app
- Built-in gallery with multi-select share and delete

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Photos, faces and videos never leave your phone. GlowCam does not use the internet.
